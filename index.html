Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Last updated 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Football President app for Android and iOS handles your information. It is a single-player club chairman game. You don’t need an account to play it, and your careers stay on your device.

At a glance

  • Personal data collected by usNone
  • Personal data shared with third partiesNone
  • Account or sign-inNone
  • Ads, analytics or trackingNone
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphoneNot used
  • Internet access on AndroidNot requested
  • Your careers, clubs and settingsOn device
  • PaymentsBy Google Play / Apple

1Who we are

Football President (the “app”) is developed and published by reqhstudio (“we”, “us”, “our”). We are responsible for the app and for this policy. Our contact details are in section 12.

2Information stored on your device

To run your career, the app keeps the following in its own private storage on your phone or tablet. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never sent to us.

  • Career saves: your club, its finances, stadium, squad, staff, season history, inbox and trophies, one database per career slot.
  • Settings: language, light or dark appearance, sound and music levels, and currency display.
  • Purchase records: which unlocks you own (for example the Plus Pack), so they work offline.
  • Error log: technical notes about crashes (no personal information), kept on the device to help if you choose to report a bug.

3Information we collect

We do not collect, store or process any personal or sensitive user data through the app. We have no servers that receive data from the app. In particular, we do not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs or IP addresses;
  • location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data;
  • usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs.

4Permissions

The Android version does not request internet access and asks for no runtime permissions. Its only system permission is the one Google Play requires for in-app purchases. The app cannot send anything from your device over the internet.

On iPhone and iPad, the app may download typeface files from Google Fonts the first time it starts and then keep them on the device. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to Google. No game data or personal information is sent with it, and we never receive it.

5Purchases

You buy the app and its in-app purchases through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, payment card or store account details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase went through and which product it was, so the app can unlock it or restore it later.

6Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer user data to anyone. The only third parties involved in using the app are the platform services you already use, each acting under its own privacy policy:

ServiceWhy it is involvedPolicy
Google PlayApp download, in-app purchases and restoring them (Android)Google Privacy Policy
Apple App StoreApp download, in-app purchases and restoring them (iOS)Apple Privacy Policy
Google FontsDownloading typefaces on iOS only (section 4)Google Fonts privacy FAQ

7Players and people in the game

Every person in the game, from players and agents to journalists and board members, has a computer-generated face and a fictional or lookalike name. If you import a community pack or rename players with the name editor, those files and edits stay on your device. We don’t upload or review them.

8Security

Your game data is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s operating system and lock screen. Because the app does not transmit your data, it is never exposed in transit or held on our systems. Emails you send us are kept in a password-protected mailbox that only we can access.

9Data retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete any career inside the app (Load Game). Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.
  • Device backups: your phone’s own backup service (Google or iCloud backup) may include the app’s save files, depending on your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and by Google or Apple.
  • Emails to us: if you contact us, we keep your email address, your message and any attachment (such as a save file) only as long as needed to answer you or fix the problem, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.
  • Accounts: the app has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to how it is used. Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask for a copy of your messages or for them to be deleted, and we will reply within 30 days.

11Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.
